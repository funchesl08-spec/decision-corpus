【文件身份】书章重印本："Availability: A heuristic for judging frequency and probability"，Amos Tversky & Daniel Kahneman。脚注自注："an abbreviated version of a paper that appeared in Cognitive Psychology, 1973, 4, 207-232"。

【定义句（OCR 照录）】
"A person is said to employ the availability heuristic whenever he estimates frequency or probability by the ease with which instances or associations could be brought to mind. To assess availability it is not necessary to perform the actual operations of retrieval or construction. It suffices to assess the ease with which these operations could be performed, much as the difficulty of a puzzle or mathematical problem can be assessed without considering specific solutions."

【规模与被试（脚注 OCR）】
"This paper explores the availability heuristic in a series of ten studies." / "Approximately 1500 subjects participated in these studies."——被试含俄勒冈大学学生（广告招募）、斯坦福大学学生（研究 8）、以色列十年级高中生（研究 5-7）。

【正文机制要点（第 2 页）】
- 可得性是"生态有效线索"：频繁的事件一般确实更容易回忆；
- 但它也受**与真实频率无关的因素**影响（媒体曝光、生动性、近期性）——这些因素一旦掺入，系统性偏差就出现；
- 例：通过回忆熟人离婚来估计离婚率——可行，但会被"谁最近离了"带偏。
